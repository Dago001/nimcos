# How to Vote — NIMCOS E-VOTING

You can vote on any smartphone, tablet or computer with internet access, during the official voting period only.

## What you need

- Your **NIS Service Number** (4 or 5 digits), as registered with NIMCOS.
- Access to the **email address** on the NIMCOS register (your one-time code is sent to it). Check your spam or junk folder if it does not arrive.

## Steps

1. **Open the voting website** given by NIMCOS and check that the address starts with `https://`.
2. **Enter your Service Number** and press **Continue**.
3. **Enter the 6-digit code** sent to your email and press **Verify**.
   - The code works once and expires after 5 minutes.
   - No code? Wait one minute and press **Resend code**. A new code cancels the old one.
4. **Read the election page** and press **Start voting**.
5. **Choose your candidates.** On a phone you see one position at a time ("Position 1 of 14"). Tap a candidate's card: it turns green and says **Selected**. Press **Next** to continue, or **Back** to change an earlier choice. On a computer, all positions appear on one page with a checklist on the left.
6. **Review your ballot.** Every position and your choice are listed. Press **Back and edit** to change anything.
7. Press **Submit final ballot**, then **Submit ballot** in the confirmation box. **After this your ballot cannot be changed.**
8. **Keep your ballot reference** (for example `NIM-2026-7KQ4M2XD`). You can check at any time on the *Verify a ballot receipt* page that it was counted.
9. Press **Finish and sign out**, especially on a shared device.

## Your vote is secret

- Officials can see *that* you voted and when, never *who you voted for*. Your choices are stored separately from your identity.
- Your receipt does not show your choices and cannot be used to prove how you voted.
- **Vote alone.** Do not let anyone watch you vote, and do not photograph your ballot.
- **Never share your code.** NIMCOS officials will never ask for it.

## Common messages

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "We could not give you access with the Service Number provided" | Check the number. If it is correct, contact the election administrator: you may not be on the register or roll. |
| "The code you entered is incorrect" | Re-enter it carefully. After 5 wrong attempts, request a new code. |
| "This code has expired" | Press **Resend code**. |
| "Your ballot session has ended" | You were inactive for too long, or signed in elsewhere. Sign in again; nothing was submitted from the expired session. |
| "You have already voted" | Each member votes once. If you did not vote, contact the election administrator immediately. |
| "Voting is not open" | Voting has not started or has closed. Check the published voting period. |

If your connection drops just after you press Submit, sign in again. If your ballot was recorded, you will see your receipt. You **cannot** accidentally vote twice.
